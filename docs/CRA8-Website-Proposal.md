## 1. What This Project Is

You asked to turn your current personal portfolio website into a company website for **CRA8**, with **a24films.com** as the inspiration.

Let me be clear about how I'm treating that inspiration: A24 is a huge studio with hundreds of films, a shop, and a membership programme. CRA8 is a young company that is just getting started, and that's exactly why this project matters. What we're borrowing from A24 is not their size, but their **presentation**: clean, cinematic, confident. A visitor landing on the CRA8 website should feel they are looking at a serious production company, whether CRA8 has three projects or three hundred.

So the plan is simple: **build a site that fits where CRA8 is today, but is structured to grow with the company.** As CRA8 adds projects, people, and announcements, the site grows with it: no rebuilding, no starting over. When CRA8 becomes as big, this same website will still carry it.

One honest note: getting there from the current site is not a small update. Because the current site is built around you as an individual, most of it (the pages, the design, the structure, and the admin area) has to be **rebuilt** around CRA8 as a company. This document lays out exactly what will be built, what you'll control yourself, and how the work runs over one month, so we're both on the same page before work begins.

## 2. The Pages Your Visitors Will See

### Home
The first thing visitors see. A bold, cinematic opening: a rotating showcase of CRA8's featured work (the same feeling as A24's homepage), followed by latest projects, latest news, and an invitation to join the mailing list. You choose what appears here and in what order. It's designed to look full and impressive even while CRA8's catalogue is still growing.

### Work (The CRA8 Slate)
A gallery of everything CRA8 has made or is making. Visitors can browse everything or filter by type: Films, Series, Documentaries, Commercials, Music, or whatever fits CRA8's work. The categories are yours to control: rename them, add new ones, or hide empty ones until there's work to show. The page looks great with five projects and scales effortlessly to fifty.

### Project Pages
Every project gets its own dedicated page, like A24 gives every film its own page. Each one can include:

- The trailer or main video
- The story / synopsis
- Credits: director, producers, cast, and crew
- A gallery of photos or stills
- Links to related CRA8 projects

### About CRA8
The company's story: what CRA8 is, why it exists, and where it's going, plus the people behind it. Even with a small founding team today, this page presents CRA8 with the weight of a real studio, and new team members can be added as the company grows.

### News
Announcements, releases, and updates from CRA8. You post news yourself whenever there's something to share: a new project, a milestone, a collaboration.

### Contact
A proper contact page with a form. Messages arrive directly in CRA8's email inbox, and enquiries can be separated into general, press, and work-with-us.

### Mailing List
A "join our mailing list" box across the site, just like A24's newsletter signup. Every visitor who signs up today is an audience member CRA8 owns forever. The full list is saved for you and you can download it anytime.

### Legal Pages
Privacy policy and terms: a standard page that makes CRA8 look properly established from day one.

## 3. What You'll Be Able to Manage Yourself

A big part of this project is the **admin area**, a private dashboard only you (and anyone you authorise) can log into. The whole point: as CRA8 grows, you update the site yourself in minutes, without needing a developer. From it you will be able to:

- **Add, edit, or remove projects** (title, video, story, credits, photos) and they appear on the site immediately
- **Choose what shows on the homepage** and in what order
- **Post news** whenever there is something to announce
- **Add or update team members** as CRA8 grows
- **See everyone who joined the mailing list** and download the list
- **Edit the menu, categories, contact details, and social media links**
- **Give access to other people at CRA8** when the team expands
- **See a history of changes**: who changed what, and when

I will personally put you through before handover, and you'll get a simple written guide to keep just in case.

## 4. Built to Grow

This is the part that matters most for a company with CRA8's ambition. The site launches fitting today's reality, but the foundation is deliberately built so these can be added later **without rebuilding anything**:

- **Online shop:** merchandise with card/transfer payments (like A24's store). This is the natural Phase 2.
- **Careers / internships page:** switched on the day CRA8 starts hiring.
- **Membership or community features:** further down the road, as the audience grows.
- **Unlimited projects, categories, team members, and news:** no limits as the catalogue expands.

**To be clear on the shop:** it is a significant piece of work on its own and is **not included** in this project's scope, price, or 1-month timeline. When CRA8 is ready for it, it will be a **separate Phase 2** with its own cost and an additional **2 to 3 weeks minimum**. I'll share that cost whenever you want to discuss it. This way the main website launches on time, and the shop is added properly when the time is right.

One thing to keep in mind for the future: as the company grows (more visitors, more videos, more photos), the database and hosting services will eventually need upgrading, which will have some cost. Just bear in mind: **growth of the platform will come with some modest monthly running costs later.**
