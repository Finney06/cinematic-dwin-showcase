# Website User Guide

## 1. Welcome

The CRA8 website introduces the studio, showcases its work and welcomes enquiries.
This guide helps you update its content, publish stories and read messages in the admin area.

## 2. A quick tour of the website

Addresses below follow your website address. The links visitors see depend on your saved **Menu** items. The standard menu is **Work**, **Services**, **Journal**, **About** and **Contact**. The logo returns visitors to Home.

| Page | Address | What visitors see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | / | A single-screen introduction: a silent opening video, then a circular image or logo, with a tagline. The circle normally links to Work. |
| Work | /work | Published projects, grouped by category, with **All** and category filters. A featured project leads the page. |
| Film | /film, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Film, at /work?category=film. |
| Television | /television, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Television. |
| Commercials | /commercials, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Commercials. |
| Music | /music, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Music. |
| Audio | /audio, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Audio. |
| Nonfiction | /nonfiction, when this category exists | Opens Work filtered to Nonfiction. |
| Project details | Open a project in Work | A project address starts with /work/. Visitors see the available description, video, stills, credits and related work. |
| Services | /services | Services, descriptions, images and capabilities, plus a link to Contact. |
| Journal | /journal | Published stories, cover images, summaries, authors and dates. Section filters appear when there is more than one section. |
| Journal entry | Open an entry in Journal | The full story, with its text and any added images, videos or links. |

| Other pages | Their saved address | The headings, text, images and links you add in **Pages**. |

Film through Nonfiction are possible categories, not six guaranteed live pages. Check **Categories** for the current names and addresses. News and Internship likewise depend on saved content and menu links. An unavailable address shows a not-found page.

## 3. Logging in to the admin area

### Sign in and sign out

**What this does:** Opens the private area where you manage the website.

**Steps**

1. Open your website address followed by /admin/login. Your developer will supply the full address.
2. Enter **Username** and **Password**, then click **Sign In**.
3. Use the left menu to choose an area. On a phone, tap the **three-line menu icon** first.
4. Click **View Site** to open the public website in another tab.
5. When finished, click **Logout** in the left menu.

**Good to know:** /admin opens the Dashboard when you are signed in. If your session expires, sign in again. Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** and enter your username. A reset link is emailed to the site's recovery inbox; it works once and expires after 15 minutes. If you still know your password, change it through **Settings** as described below.

## 4. The Dashboard at a glance

**What this does:** Shows enquiries waiting to be read, unfinished drafts and recently edited projects.

**Steps**

1. Click **Dashboard**.
2. If an unread-enquiry notice appears, click **Read →**.
3. Choose **Add a project**, **Write a journal entry** or **Edit a page** to start work.
4. Open a draft or a project under **Recently edited** to continue. **All projects →** opens the complete project list.

**Good to know:** The Dashboard shows up to four drafts and four recently edited projects. A Journal draft link opens the Journal list; select the entry there. Empty sections may not appear.

## 5. Everyday tasks

### Undo and redo an edit

**What this does:** Steps backwards or forwards through changes in the editor you currently have open.

**Steps**

1. While editing, click the curved **Undo** arrow near the CRA8 name. On a phone, it is in the top bar.
2. Click **Redo** to restore the change you just undid.
3. Check the result, then use that editor's save button.

**Good to know:** These controls work in the Project, Journal, Services, Team, Categories, About, Hero, Pages and general Settings editors. They do not reverse deletions, list reordering, menu changes, message actions or password changes. Grey arrows mean no history is available. Leaving an editor or reloading can clear its history. Undo is not a backup. Save before changing screens; do not rely on a warning to protect your work.

### Projects: add or edit work

**What this does:** Adds or updates a project in Work and its own detail page.

**Steps**

1. Open **Projects**. Click **+ New Project**, or find a project with **Search projects…** and click its title or **Edit**.
2. For unfinished work, switch **Published** to **Draft**. New projects start with **Published** selected.
3. Complete **Title**, **Category** and **Year**. These are required.
4. Add **Status**, **CRA8 credits**, **Logline**, **Short description** and **Synopsis** as needed. A logline is a one-sentence introduction.
5. Add **Full film (YouTube)** and, if wanted, **Trailer (YouTube)**. Paste the full YouTube links. The trailer plays silently behind the title; use a short trailer there.
6. Add **Thumbnail**. If a YouTube picture appears, **Use as thumbnail →** copies it. Under **Stills**, use **+ Still** for each extra picture.
7. Complete **Director**, **Producers**, **Cast** and **Other credits** where needed. **+ Credit** adds another credit.
8. Optionally use **Feature this project**, add **Extra content**, and write a **Preview description** for search results and shared links.
9. Click **Create project** for new work or **Save changes** for existing work. Open **View Site**, then **Work**, to check it.

**Good to know:** Only one project is explicitly featured at a time. **Status** is descriptive text; it does not publish the project. Draft project addresses show “not found” to visitors. The form returns to the project list after saving.

### Projects: reorder, hide or delete

**What this does:** Changes the order or visibility of existing projects.

**Steps**

1. Open **Projects** and clear **Search projects…**. Choose **All** for a clear view of the whole order.
2. Drag a project's **≡** handle up or down. Wait for **Reordered**. Reordering also works within a category filter.
3. To hide a project, click its **Unpublish** switch in the list. To show it again, click **Publish**. These changes save immediately.
4. To permanently remove a project, click **Delete**, read the confirmation, then click **Delete** again. Choose **Cancel** to keep it.

**Good to know:** Work groups projects by category and gives the featured project priority, so it is not a single flat list. Use unpublish when you may need the project again. There is no restore bin.

### Hero: change the homepage introduction

**What this does:** Changes the homepage circle, opening clip, tagline and glow.

**Steps**

1. Open **Hero**.
2. Update **Tagline (Bottom text)**. **Brand Text** supplies the heading read aloud by accessibility tools and the video title; it does not replace the visible logo.
3. Add **Hero Video (plays first)** using a short upload or a YouTube link.
4. Add **Hero Circle Image (shown after video ends)** if you want a picture instead of the default logo.
5. Set **Circle Link URL** to the destination address. URL means a web address. Leave it empty to link to /work.
6. Choose **Hero Atmosphere** and adjust **Glow Brightness** if wanted. **Off** removes the glow.
7. Check **Preview**, click **Save Changes**, then reopen Home to check the clip and link.

**Good to know:** The preview shows the settled circle, not the whole opening sequence. Uploaded clips play to their end; YouTube clips show for about eight seconds. The opening is silent. Visitors who reduce motion may see the still immediately. Clearing the video restores the default clip; clearing the image restores the default logo.

### About: update the studio and founder

**What this does:** Changes the main About page story and pictures.

**Steps**

1. Open **About**. Update **Hero Image**, **Title** and **Subtitle**.
2. Edit **Intro (Large text)** and **Bio Paragraphs**. Use **+ Add paragraph** for another paragraph or **×** beside one to remove it.
3. Set **Image 1** and **Image 2** under **Gallery Images**.
4. Use **+ Add section** under **Additional Sections** for more writing. Complete **Section heading** and **Section text...**.
5. If needed, add **Full Width Image** and the founder's **Name**, **Bio**, **Role / Credits** and **Founder Portrait**.
6. For a founder link, complete **CTA Label** and **CTA URL**. CTA means a button or link inviting visitors to take an action.
7. Click **Save Changes**, then check **About** on the public site.

**Good to know:** The public gallery shows only the first two filled pictures, even though **+ Add image** allows more. With one picture, it repeats that picture. The founder link appears only when the founder section has a name or bio, and both link fields are filled. Team members have their own editor.

### Team: manage people on About

**What this does:** Adds or updates the people shown under The Studio on About.

**Steps**

1. Open **Team** and click **+ New Member**, or click an existing person's name or **Edit**.
2. Complete **Name**, **Role**, **Bio** and **Portrait**. Add social or other links under **Links** with **+ Link**.
3. Click **Save**, then **Back** to return to the list.
4. Drag **≡** to change the order. It saves immediately.
5. To remove someone, click **Delete** in the list, then **Delete** in the confirmation.

**Good to know:** Members go live when saved; there is no draft switch. Removing all members hides the team section. **Discard** abandons unsaved editor changes.

### Journal: publish studio news or a story

**What this does:** Adds writing to Journal and creates an individual story page.

**Steps**

1. Open **Journal**, then **+ New Entry**, or select an entry to edit.
2. Complete **Title**, **Section**, **Author** and **Date**. Keep section names consistent, such as “Behind the Scenes”.
3. Leave **URL** empty to make an address from the title, or enter the desired address ending.
4. Write **Standfirst**: one or two sentences introducing the story. Add **Cover image**.
5. Build the story under **The entry** using the content tools below. Optionally select **Feature this entry** and fill **Preview title** and **Preview description**.
6. Click **Save** to keep it as a draft. When ready, change **Draft** to **Published** and click **Save** again.
7. Use **View live →** beside its address to check the saved story. Return with **Back**.
8. In the list, use **Publish** or **Unpublish** for an immediate visibility change. Use **Delete** and confirm **Delete** only for permanent removal.

**Good to know:** New entries start as drafts. Featured entries lead, then newer dates; dragging **≡** changes order only where those priorities match. **Date** is not a scheduled publishing control. Changing **URL** breaks old story links. A draft's live link is not a private preview.

### Services: update what the studio offers

**What this does:** Changes the list on Services.

**Steps**

1. Open **Services** and click **+ New Service**, or select a service to edit.
2. Complete **Service**, **Summary**, **Description** and optional **Image**. Leave **URL key** blank to make it from the service name.
3. Under **Capabilities**, click **+ Capability** for each skill or offering. Use **×** to remove a row.
4. Click **Save**, then **Back**. Drag **≡** in the list to change the public order.
5. To remove a service, click **Delete**, then confirm **Delete**.

**Good to know:** Saved services go live immediately; there is no draft switch. The main Services heading and introduction are edited in **Pages**. A service's URL key does not create a separate service detail page.

### Categories: organise your work

**What this does:** Creates or updates the category choices used by Projects and Work.

**Steps**

1. Open **Categories**, then **+ New Category**, or click a category to edit it.
2. Enter **Label**, such as “Film”. Leave **URL** empty for a new category to make it from the label.
3. Add **Description** and optional **Hero image** for its filtered Work view.
4. Click **Save**. Assign projects to it through each project's **Category** field.
5. Drag **≡** in the category list to reorder categories. The change saves immediately.
6. To delete a category, first move all its projects, including drafts, to another category. Then click **Delete** and confirm **Delete**.

**Good to know:** Empty categories stay out of Work's category choices. There is no category hide switch. Main menu links are managed separately in **Menu**. Changing **URL** changes the category address; check existing links afterwards. **Manage them →** opens its projects.

### Pages: edit page text or create a page

**What this does:** Changes page headings, introductions, extra content and shared-link previews.

**Steps**

1. Open **Pages** and select a page from the left list.
2. Edit **Heading**, **Lead paragraph** and **Page content**. Custom pages also have **Hero image**.
3. Optionally set **Preview title**, **Preview description** and **Preview image**.
4. For Contact, use **Form is on** / **Form is off** to show or hide the form. Under **Enquiry types**, use **+ Enquiry type** to add a choice to its dropdown.
5. Click **Save changes •**. When saved, the button reads **Saved**. Use **View live →** to check the page.
6. For a new page, click **+ New Page**. Fill **Title** and check **Address** carefully. Choose **Add to the menu** if wanted. Keep **Start as a draft** while preparing it, then click **Create page**.
7. Add content, change **Draft** to **Published** when ready, and click **Save changes •**. Check its menu link separately in **Menu**.
8. To remove a custom page, click **Delete this page** and confirm **Delete**. Built-in pages cannot be deleted.

**Good to know:** Projects, services and stories have their own editors, as does **About**. Older News or Internship text may need your developer's help to edit. Saving a page as **Draft** hides its exact menu link. Publishing it again does not restore that link: use **Show in the menu** in **Menu**. Check the direct address too. The Journal landing page does not disappear when set to draft; unpublish individual entries to hide those stories.

### Content tools: add text, pictures and links

**What this does:** Builds **Page content**, **The entry** or **Extra content** from small sections called blocks.

**Steps**

1. Click **+ Text** and type your paragraphs. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
2. Add other sections as needed: **+ Heading**, **+ Pull quote**, **+ Image**, **+ Gallery**, **+ Video**, **+ Button**, **+ Two columns** or **+ Spacer**.
3. Fill the new section. A video takes a **YouTube link or video URL**. A button needs both **Label** and its destination address. Use **+ Image** within a gallery for each picture.
4. Use **↑** (**Move up**) or **↓** (**Move down**) to reorder a section. Use **×** (**Remove block**) to remove it.
5. Save the parent page, entry or project with its save button.

**Good to know:** Content sections use arrows, not dragging. Empty sections usually do not appear publicly. **Spacer** adds space; **sm**, **md** and **lg** mean small, medium and large.

### Menu: change navigation links

**What this does:** Changes the links in the public menu and page footer.

**Steps**

1. Open **Menu** and click **+ Add Item**.
2. Enter **Label** and **Path**, such as /contact. Click **Add Item**.
3. To change an existing link, click **Edit**, update **Label** and the address box marked **/path**, then click **Save**.
4. Drag **≡** to reorder links. Use **Hide from the menu** or **Show in the menu** to change visibility. These changes save immediately.
5. To remove a link, click **Delete**, then confirm **Delete**.
6. Refresh the public site and test the link.

**Good to know:** Removing or hiding a link does not delete or hide its destination page. Adding a link does not fill a page with content. If no visible menu items remain, the standard menu returns automatically.

### Messages: read and reply to enquiries

**What this does:** Opens enquiries submitted through Contact.

**Steps**

1. Open **Messages**. Select **Unread**, **Read**, **Archived** or **All**.
2. Click a message to open it. Opening an unread message marks it as read; switch to **Read** or **All** if it leaves the Unread list.
3. Click **Reply by email** to open your email app. Write and send the reply there.
4. Use **Archive** to put it aside, **Unarchive** to return it to Read, or **Mark unread** as a reminder.
5. Only when no longer needed, click **Delete** and confirm **Delete**.

**Good to know:** Enquiries are kept here, not automatically emailed to you. Changing **Contact Email** does not forward them. This list loads the latest 100 enquiries and has no older-message button; ask your developer if an older message is missing.

### Settings: update contact details and appearance

**What this does:** Changes details used across the site.

**Steps**

1. Open **Settings**. Update **Site Title**, **Site Description**, **Default share image**, **Contact Email**, **Phone**, **Studio location** or **Copyright Text** as needed.
2. Under **Social Links**, use **+ Add social link**. Complete **Label (e.g. TikTok)** and the **https://...** address box. Use **Remove** for an unwanted link.
3. If changing the design, choose **Display Font Family** for headings and **Body Font Family** for other text.
4. For movement, adjust **Animation Enabled**, **Animation Style** or **Animation Speed**. **Section Controls** lets you adjust individual areas; **Profile** chooses their style, and **Duration**, **Delay** and **Distance** control timing and movement.
5. Click **Save**, then check the public site on a phone and a larger screen.

**Good to know:** Either Settings **Save** button saves general settings and social links together. Keep a note of appearance settings before experimenting. Some visitors' devices reduce motion automatically. Changing **Site Title** does not replace the logo.

### Settings: change your password

**What this does:** Replaces your admin password.

**Steps**

1. Open **Settings** and find **Change Password**.
2. Enter **Current Password**, **New Password** and **Confirm Password**.
3. Click **Change Password** and wait for **Password changed!**.
4. Keep the new password in your password manager.

**Good to know:** The new entries must match. The screen requires at least six characters; use a longer, unique password. This action has its own button and cannot be undone with **Undo**.

## 6. Images and videos

### Prepare and add media

**What this does:** Keeps pictures sharp and pages comfortable to load.

**Steps**

1. Prepare a copy of your original using the starting sizes below. These are preparation targets, not required dimensions.
2. In an image field, click **Drop file here or click to upload**, or drag one picture onto it. Alternatively, use **Or paste URL...** for a publicly accessible picture address.
3. Wait for **Uploading...** to finish and check the picture appears. **Click to change** replaces it; **Remove current file** clears that field.
4. Save the page or item containing the picture.
5. Check it on a phone and a larger screen. Keep important faces and text away from edges that may be cropped.

| Use | Starting size or format |
| --- | --- |
| Project thumbnail, still or service image | Around 1920 × 1080 pixels, wide |
| Large page image | Around 2000–2200 pixels wide; allow room for cropping |
| Team or founder portrait | Around 800 × 1000 pixels, upright |
| About gallery | Around 900 × 1200 pixels, upright |
| Homepage circle image | Around 1200 × 1200 pixels; keep the subject central |
| Default share image | 1200 × 630 pixels, as the Settings screen suggests |
| Photos | JPG or WebP; PNG is also accepted. Aim for under 500 KB to 1 MB where the picture still looks good. |
| Opening video | MP4 or WebM; the Hero screen suggests 4–12 seconds. Keep it short and compressed. |

**Good to know:** Uploads accept JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, MP4, WebM and MOV. SVG and OGG uploads are not accepted. The default upload limit is 25 MB per item; your developer must confirm the live limit. Uploaded JPG, PNG and WebP pictures are automatically reduced to at most 2200 pixels wide and compressed. Videos and pasted picture links do not receive that treatment. Keep originals safely elsewhere. Use YouTube links for full films, and check that visitors can watch them without signing in. The admin's video preview may not display a YouTube link correctly; test it on the public page.

## 7. Do's and don'ts

- Do save and check each change before starting another task.
- Do keep original pictures, videos and important copy outside the website.
- Do use drafts or archive where available when you may need content again.
- Don't delete projects, stories or messages expecting **Undo** to restore them.
- Don't change established addresses without checking links that use them.
- Don't remove Contact or all menu links by accident.
- Don't share your password or upload material you do not have permission to publish.
- Don't change unfamiliar appearance settings during a routine text update.

## 8. Troubleshooting

1. **My change isn't showing →** Check that you clicked the correct save button and saw success. Refresh the public page. Check the item's **Published** setting and any menu visibility switch.
2. **My project or story says “not found” →** Check that it is published, saved and still exists. Open it from Work or Journal to get its current address.
3. **I cannot reorder projects →** Clear **Search projects…**, then drag **≡**. For Journal, featured status and date take priority over dragging.
4. **My upload did nothing →** Check the format and reduce the size. Wait for the picture to appear before saving. If it still fails, ask your developer to check uploads.
5. **My video will not play →** Open the YouTube link yourself and check public viewing is allowed. Use the full link in the appropriate field. Silent homepage playback is expected.
6. **A contact enquiry is missing from my email →** Look in **Messages**. Enquiries are not automatically emailed. Use **All** if opening the message moved it out of **Unread**.
7. **Reply by email does nothing →** Set up your normal email app, or copy the sender's address and reply manually from your mailbox.
8. **The menu link is missing or empty →** Check **Menu**, its visibility switch and its **Path**. Confirm the destination has saved content. An empty category does not appear among Work's category choices.
9. **I was signed out or saving fails →** Keep a copy of unsaved writing, check your connection and sign in again. Use **Try again** where offered. Send the exact error to your developer if it continues.
10. **A page remains accessible after I set it to draft →** Check **Menu** for links that were added or shown again, and hide them. Journal's landing page has a known visibility limitation; hide individual entries with **Unpublish** and ask your developer about the landing page.
