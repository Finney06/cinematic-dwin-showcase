1.What visitors see

  • Home: a short silent video, then the CRA8 circle and tagline. Tapping the circle opens Work.
  • Work: all your projects, with filters such as Film, Television and Commercials. Each project has its own page with the video, stills and credits.
  • Services, Journal and About: what the studio offers, your stories, and the studio and team.
  • Contact: your contact details and a message form.

2.Getting into the admin

  • Go to and bookmark it. Nothing on the website links to it.
  • Type your Username and Password, then tap Sign In.
  • On a phone, the menu is behind the button at the top.
  • View Site opens your website in a new tab. Logout signs you out.
  • This guide is always in the admin under User Guide.
Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, click Forgot password? and enter your username. A reset link is emailed to the site's recovery inbox. It works once and expires after 15 minutes.

3.Dashboard

The first screen after you sign in.

  • New messages waiting for you. Tap Read → to open them.
  • Shortcuts: Add a project, Write a journal entry, Edit a page.
  • Your unfinished drafts and recently edited projects.

4.Projects

  • + New Project, or tap a project to change it.
  • Fill in Title, Category and Year. These are required.
  • Full film (YouTube): paste the YouTube link. Trailer (YouTube): a short clip that plays silently behind the title.
  • Thumbnail is the main picture. Add more pictures with + Still.
  • Add Director, Producers and Cast. Use + Credit for anyone else.
  • Feature this project puts it first on Work.
  • Tap Create project or Save changes when you're done.

In the project list:

  • Drag the ≡ handle to change the order.
  • Unpublish hides a project and Publish shows it again.
Hiding is better than deleting. A deleted project can't be brought back.

5.Home page

Open Hero in the admin.

  • Tagline: the short line under the circle.
  • Hero Video: the short clip that plays first. Keep it 4–12 seconds.
  • Hero Circle Image: the picture in the circle after the video. Leave it empty to show the CRA8 logo.
  • Circle Link URL: where the circle goes when tapped. Leave it empty to open Work.
  • Hero Atmosphere and Glow Brightness set the soft glow. Off removes it.
  • Tap Save Changes.

6.About & Team

About has everything on the About page: the heading, introduction, your story, two gallery photos and the founder. Tap Save Changes when you're done.

Team is where you add the people shown on the About page:

  • + New Member, then add a name, role, short bio and photo. Tap Save.
  • Drag ≡ to change the order.
The About page shows only the first two gallery photos.

7.Journal

Your news and behind-the-scenes stories.

  • + New Entry, then add a Title, Author, Date and Cover image.
  • Standfirst: one or two sentences that introduce the story.
  • Write the story under The entry (see "Adding text and pictures" below).
  • New entries start as Draft, so they don't show on the website. Switch to Published and tap Save when it's ready.
Adding text and pictures. Journal entries, pages and projects are built from blocks. Tap + Text, + Heading, + Image, + Gallery, + Video or + Button to add one. Use ↑ ↓ to move a block and × to remove it.

8.Services

  • + New Service, or tap one to change it.
  • Add a name, a short Summary, a Description and a photo.
  • Use + Capability for each thing the service includes.
  • Tap Save. Drag ≡ in the list to change the order.

9.Categories

The groups your projects sit in, such as Film or Commercials.

  • + New Category, then add a Label and Description.
  • Drag ≡ to change the order of the filters on Work.
  • A category with no projects doesn't show on the website.
  • To delete a category, first move its projects to another category.

10.Pages & Menu

Pages changes the heading and text at the top of each page.

  • Pick a page on the left, make your changes, then tap Save changes.
  • On Contact, you can turn the message form on or off.
  • + New Page makes a new page. Tick Add to the menu to link to it.

Menu changes the links at the top of the website.

  • + Add Item, then type the name and the page address, for example /contact.
  • Drag ≡ to reorder. Hide from the menu removes a link without deleting the page.

11.Messages

Messages sent through the Contact form.

  • Tap a message to open it.
  • Reply by email opens your email app with the reply ready to write.
  • Archive tidies it away and Mark unread keeps it as a reminder.
Every message is also emailed to . Tap Reply in that email to answer the sender directly.
Messages are not sent to your email yet. Check Messages in the admin regularly.

12.Settings

  • Your Contact Email, Phone and Studio location, as shown to visitors.
  • Social Links: tap + Add social link, then add a name and a link.
  • Fonts and animations: make one small change at a time and check the website afterwards.
  • Change Password: type your current password, then the new one twice.
  • Tap Save.

13.Photos & videos

Project picturesWide, about 1920 × 1080
PortraitsUpright, about 800 × 1000
Home page circleSquare, about 1200 × 1200, subject in the middle
Home page videoMP4, 4–12 seconds
Full filmsUpload to YouTube, then paste the link

Photos are made smaller for you when you upload them. Keep your originals somewhere safe.

14.Good to know

  • Always save before you leave a screen. Undo only works on the screen you're on.
  • After saving, open View Site and refresh to check your change.
  • Hide things instead of deleting them. Deleting can't be undone.
  • Never share your password.
  • If something looks broken, take a screenshot and send it to your developer with what you were trying to do.
